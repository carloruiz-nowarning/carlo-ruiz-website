Our Roots

Who Are We?

The Happy Camper is a community-centric cannabis dispensary in Charlotte, NC, dedicated to offering high-quality cannabis products. Located in Optimist Park, we recently opened our Brick and Mortar store, and we also travel around Charlotte and the East Coast, vending out of a charming 1960’s Shasta camper.

At The Happy Camper, we provide an extensive selection of cannabis products, including smokable, edible, tincture and topical options that feature various cannabinoids, such as THCa, Delta 9, CBN, CBD, Delta 8, HHC and more.

Beyond simply selling products, we deeply care about creating an exceptional experience for our customers. We foster a sense of community among individuals who share a passion for leading holistic lifestyles and advocating for their beliefs. Our mission is to bring joy, health, and wellness through cannabis-based education and products, all while cultivating an atmosphere of positivity for every individual who visits our camper or store.
